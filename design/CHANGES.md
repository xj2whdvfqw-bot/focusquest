# FocusQuest: design changes to port into SwiftUI

Source of truth: `design/focusquest.html` (the HTML prototype). When a detail below is unclear, open that file and read the matching JavaScript or CSS. Every value here comes from it.

Work through the sections in order. Each one is self-contained, so you can build and test on the iPhone (GitHub Actions → IPA → Sideloadly) after each section.

Colours are given as hex or as `rgba(r,g,b,a)` with 0–255 channels. Sizes are in points (1 CSS px = 1 pt).

---

## 0. Global tokens

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `cta` | `#FFD83D` on text `#2A1E00` | Main action buttons only (Fight, Start Battle, Next boss) |
| `accent` | `rgb(107,230,199)` | Progress, success, ticks |
| `danger` | `rgb(255,84,102)` | Retreat, delete, angry boss |
| `card` | `rgba(13,11,28,0.60)` + blur 16 | Glass cards everywhere |
| `card` on bright backgrounds | `rgba(13,11,28,0.64)`, cards at `.40` opacity + blur 16 | See section 12 |
| `text2` | white 78 % | Secondary text |
| `text3` | white 50 % | Tertiary text |

- Pixel font (`Pixelify Sans`) only for screen titles (`h1`), with weight 500, tracking +5 %, word spacing +12 %. Everything else uses the rounded system font (`.rounded` design).
- Secondary text minimum 13 pt.
- Remove all decorative icons: no map icon on world cards, no icons next to subjects, no icons in stat tiles.
- Content scrolls under the status bar and Dynamic Island. Do **not** clip it at the safe area. Put a gradient behind the status bar: `rgba(6,5,16,.55)` → transparent over 84 pt.

## 1. Tab bar

- 5 tabs: **Arena, Path, Shop, Stats, Hero**. Stats is its own tab now (it used to live inside Camp/Profile).
- Icons only, no labels (keep labels as accessibility labels). Icon size 26.
- Arena icon: outlined house with an arched door. Hero icon: outlined person inside a circle. Path, Shop and Stats keep their current filled icons.
- Hide the tab bar completely during a battle.
- Shop tab is locked until the player has **3 wins**: show it at 45 % opacity with a small `🔒 1/3` badge. Tapping it shows a toast "The Shop opens after 3 wins (1/3)".
- Gradient under the tab bar so content fades out: transparent → `rgba(6,5,16,.92)` at 45 % → `#060510` at 70 %, height = tab bar height + bottom inset + 36.

## 2. Arena

- Title "Ready to focus?" (no small label above it).
- Streak pill (flame + number + "days streak") at the top left.
- **No Hero HP bar.** Hero HP is removed from the whole app.
- Centre: "Continue your path", large boss sprite (128 pt) without a card or circle behind it, boss name, and below it **"Boss 3 of 4 · Villain Vault"** (never "Node 3").
- Bottom (thumb zone): yellow button **"▶ Fight · 20 min"**, rounded font, sentence case. Below it a text link **"Not feeling it? Just 5 minutes"**.
- Remove: the Custom Focus card and "See all worlds".

### 2a. Easter egg: poke the boss

Tapping the boss sprite shows a speech bubble above it for about 2.2 s, with a little bob animation.

- Normal lines (pick at random, never the same twice in a row; `{m}` = boss minutes):
  "Oh, you again? Your notes miss you." · "I've napped through {m} minutes before. Try me." · "Bet you'll check your phone in 3 minutes." · "Studying later? That's my favourite word." · "I feed on open tabs. Keep them coming." · "Psst. Just one more video. Trust me." · "You won't last {m} minutes. Nobody does." · "Phone down? Ha. Prove it."
- **4 or more taps within 1.5 s each** make the boss angry: it shakes, gets a soft red radial glow behind it (no hard edge), plays a hit sound, and shows a red bubble with one of:
  "STOP. POKING. ME." · "Poke me once more and I'm adding 5 minutes." · "You poke like you study. Badly." · "Enough! Fight me if you dare."
- Bubble style = pixel-art bubble (see section 11). Angry variant has a `danger` fill and white text.

### 2b. Easter egg: streak on fire

- Every tap on the streak pill makes it bump (scale 1.06).
- **5 taps within 600 ms each** set the pill on fire. Restarting while it burns must work, and it must never get stuck (always clean up after about 4.5 s).
- Pixel-art fire, 3 pt pixel grid, palette `#FFF6C4 #FFD64A #FF961E #EB5016 #A02412`:
  - Ignition starts at the **top centre** and runs **clockwise** around the pill outline in 0.9 s, then burns for 1.5 s and fades over 0.8 s.
  - Particles spawn along the lit part of the outline (more at the burning front) and move outward and up. Young particles are 3×3 pixels with a light core, later 2×2, then 1×1, getting darker.
  - A 1-pixel ember line follows the outline with a running yellow/orange flicker.
  - Pill gets a `0 0 0 3 rgba(235,80,22,.55)` outline while burning. Plays the victory jingle.
- Implement with SwiftUI `Canvas` + `TimelineView`. Reference: `streakFire()` in the HTML.

## 3. Path

- Title "Choose a world". **All worlds start collapsed**; a world opens when its card is tapped.
- World card: title, tagline, "2 / 4 cleared", progress bar in the world colour. No icon.
- Boss tiles in a 3-column grid: number badge, sprite, name (max 2 lines), and the length "20 min". **No highlighted "next" boss** (no coloured border, no red label).
- If the boss was beaten: a small green pill on the sprite with ✓ and the win count, e.g. **"✓ 3×"** (count from the battle log, minimum 1 for beaten bosses).
- Boss length is fixed: `minutes = 10 + 5 × index` (index from 0).
- The Victory screen must show the boss you **actually** beat, even though the path moves on (store `lastBeaten` before advancing).

## 4. Fight sheet (opens from Fight on Arena or a boss tile)

- Close button (✕) top right. Swipe down also closes.
- Title "Fight {boss name}".
- **Studying:** row of subject chips + "+ Subject" + "Edit" link at the right of the label.
  - Chips keep a **fixed order** (the order of the subject list). Selecting a chip only changes its highlight and never moves it.
  - Show the first 5. If the selected one is further down, show it in 5th place. "More…" expands the full list.
  - The last used subject is preselected (stored).
- **Sound:** chips Off · Lo-fi · Brown noise · White noise.
- **Selected chip style** (all chips app-wide): background `rgba(0,0,0,.55)`, white bold text, 1.5 pt inner border `rgba(255,216,61,.6)`. No yellow or mint fill.
- **No length picker.** The boss length is fixed. One big yellow button **"▶ Start 20 min Battle"** (height 72, font 22 bold).

### 4a. Adding subjects (lazy-student flow, 1–2 taps)

- Tapping "+ Subject" turns that chip into an inline text field (no modal), with suggestions: Math, English, Economics, Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, Accounting, Statistics, Finance, Marketing, Management, Law, Programming, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, History, Geography, German, Czech, Spanish, French, Philosophy, Psychology, Design.
- Return or a tapped suggestion creates the subject **and selects it**.
- Duplicate check (case- and accent-insensitive, prefix of 4+ letters): pick the existing one and show the toast "You already have Math. Picked it."
- Long-press a chip, or tap "Edit subjects" in Stats, to open **Your subjects** (section 7).
- "Edit" mode in the fight sheet: chips show a red ✕. The first tap turns the chip red with "Delete Math?", the second tap deletes it (soft delete, section 7).

## 5. Battle

- Placing step: "Lay the phone face-down. The battle starts when it lies still." The battle starts on the motion sensor (no 1.6 s timer).
- Card: boss sprite, name, "Novice · 10 min", big timer, a thin boss HP bar **without** a number, and the status line "Phone down. The boss is losing." Remove: combo, "13 HP left", the tasks list, and the top-right buttons (rules and music).
- **Pause once:** a single pause per battle that lasts at most **2:00**, with a live countdown "Paused · back in 1:43 or the boss escapes". When it runs out, the boss escapes.
- Retreat → confirmation: "Don't let {boss} win" / "The boss escapes: no XP and no coins for this battle" / buttons "Stay and Fight", "Retreat Anyway".
- Bosses breathe (scale 1.02) and **never** fade. Their opacity stays 1.
- Round buttons on bright backgrounds: disc fill `rgba(13,11,28,.55)` (danger `rgba(60,8,20,.6)`, accent `rgba(8,40,34,.6)`), shadow `0 8 22 rgba(0,0,0,.45)`, 1 pt inner border, white captions with a strong shadow.

### 5a. Phone lifted

- Static dark red `#4A0710`. **No strobe.** Only the countdown number pulses slowly (opacity 1 → .55, 2.4 s). No animation with Reduce Motion.
- Text: "Put it back on the desk" / "Lay it face-down. Your first slip in a battle is forgiven."
- **No "I put it back" button.** The alarm stops when the sensor says the phone is still.
- **First lift per battle is forgiven:** the count shows 0 and the text reads "Slip forgiven" / "Nothing lost this time. Next slip and the boss escapes." On the second lift the boss escapes.

### 5b. No hero HP, no death

Remove Hero HP, potions (Health, Time-Freeze), relics, combo, the slot machine and the village. The only penalty is **"the boss escapes"**: no XP and no coins for that battle. The streak and coins are never taken away.

Defeat screen: title "{boss} escaped", reason ("You retreated." / "The phone stayed up." / "The pause ran out."), "No loot this time, your streak and coins are safe.", button "Back".

## 6. Victory and break

### Victory (must actually pay out)

- XP `= minutes × 12`, coins `= minutes × 3`. Add them, level up when XP ≥ XP needed.
- Streak +1 once per calendar day.
- Append to the battle log (boss, date, xp, minutes) and add minutes and battles to the subject stats (or "General" if no subject).
- If the boss was the current frontier boss, advance the world and show "Next up: {next boss} · {min} min".
- Show the beaten boss sprite (no trophy overlay). XP and coins count up over 0.9 s (ease-out).
- After a 5-minute quick battle, also show "Keep going · 20 min battle".

### Break (after Collect Loot)

Bottom sheet:

- "Break" label, a big thin timer **5:00 that really counts down**, and "Stand up, drink some water. The next boss waits." At 0:00 it shows "Break's over" with the timer in yellow, plus a sound.
- Main button (yellow, 3 parts): ▶ disc · "NEXT BOSS" small caps + boss name (truncate) · "25 min" pill.
- Two tiles: **Same again** (last beaten boss, opens its fight sheet) and **Change boss** ("Pick on the Path", opens the Path tab).
- "Done for today" outlined button, compact (not full width).

### "Just 5 minutes"

A quick 5-minute battle against the current world's boss. It does not move the Path.

## 7. Your subjects (manager sheet)

- Rows: editable name field · **Hide/Show** · trash icon.
- **Hide:** the subject disappears from the fight sheet **and** from Stats. Its data is kept. The row turns grey with strikethrough and the button reads "Show".
- **Delete:** the row turns red asking "Delete Math? You can restore it for 24 hours." with **Cancel** and **Delete**.
  - On delete: the subject disappears from fights and Stats and moves to **Recently deleted** ("Gone for good in 23 h, with its stats", **Restore**).
  - After 24 h the subject and its stats are purged.
  - A toast with Undo appears for 5 s.
- Header text: "Hidden subjects disappear from fights and Stats. Nothing is lost, tap Show to bring them back."
- "+ Subject" at the bottom, compact "✓ Done" button.
- On launch, any subject that has stats but is missing from the list must be added back (data repair).

## 11. Toasts and speech bubbles (pixel-art bubble)

- Cream fill `#FFFAF0`, 3 pt dark outline `#1B1630`, **stepped pixel corners** (4 pt steps), and a hard drop shadow `0 4 0 rgba(10,8,24,.45)`.
- Text: rounded font, 15 pt, weight 800, colour `#1B1630`, tabular numbers. **Not** the pixel font (numbers must be readable).
- Toasts sit at the top centre with width fitting the content. They appear with a 3-step slide down. Undo is in `#B4530A`.
- Boss speech bubbles use the same shape plus a stepped tail at the bottom centre. The angry variant has a `danger` fill and white text.

Bright backgrounds are Daybreak, Sunset, Pyramids, Farm and Wind Farm (`isBright`).

- Daybreak, Pyramids, Farm and Wind Farm also darken the top and bottom of the screen (scrim) and drop the circle behind the Arena boss (a drop shadow under the sprite instead).

## 13. Settings

1. **Sounds & notifications** (bell icon) opens a **separate page** with "‹ Settings" back. It contains:
   - **Battle sound:** Off / Lo-fi / Brown noise / White noise.
   - **Sound effects:** Tap and battle sounds · Achievement sounds · Alarm sound · Vibrate on the alarm.
   - **Notifications:** Daily reminder (17:00) · Battle finished · Break is over.
2. **Background:** current name, opens Shop › Backgrounds. Hidden while the Shop is locked (it was a dead end).
3. **Feedback:** Contact the developer (mail icon), Rate this app (star icon).
4. Footer: "FocusQuest version 1.0.0", then "Privacy Policy · Terms of Use".
5. Compact "✓ Done" button.

Icons are outlined, 24 pt, without a background box.

### Audio rules

- Exactly one track at a time: call `stopAllAudio()` before starting another track.
- Lo-fi without the hiss layer underneath.
- Brown noise gain 0.17, white noise gain 0.04 (about 65 % quieter than before).
- The toggles really mute their sounds. With Alarm sound off, the phone only vibrates.
