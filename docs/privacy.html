FocusQuest

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

FocusQuest does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you create stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your hero, subjects, focus history, level, coins, backgrounds and settings are saved only on your device. FocusQuest has no accounts and no server, so this data is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Motion sensor

During a battle, FocusQuest reads your iPhone's motion sensor to check that the phone is lying face-down and still. The readings are processed in real time on your device. They are not saved and never leave your iPhone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, FocusQuest schedules them on your iPhone: when a battle ends, when a break is over, and an optional daily reminder. No push server is involved. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iPhone Settings.

No tracking, analytics or ads

FocusQuest does not track you, does not use analytics or advertising, and contains no third-party SDKs.

Contacting us

If you write to us, for example through the Support page, we use your message only to reply to you.

Deleting your data

Open FocusQuest, then Settings › Reset progress to delete everything. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

FocusQuest collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
