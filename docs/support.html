Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? We're happy to help.

Report a bug or suggest an idea: open an issue on GitHub.

How does a battle work?

Pick a subject and a boss, then put your iPhone face-down on the desk. While the phone lies still, the boss loses HP. Beat it to earn XP and coins.

I picked up my phone. What happens?

You get 10 seconds to put it back down. The first slip in a battle is forgiven. After the second, the boss escapes and the battle earns nothing, but you never lose your streak or coins.

What if I get a call during a battle?

The battle pauses automatically for as long as the call lasts. It doesn't use your one pause, and picking up the phone doesn't count as a slip. When the call ends, put the phone face-down again and the battle continues.

The battle doesn't start when the phone is on the desk

Make sure the phone lies flat and face-down. If the motion sensor still doesn't react, use the option to start without the sensor on the battle screen.

I don't get notifications

Open FocusQuest › Settings › Sounds & notifications. If it says notifications are off, tap Open Settings and allow them for FocusQuest.

What happens if I close the app during a battle?

Locking the phone or switching apps is fine, the battle keeps running. If the app is closed completely, that battle is lost.

How do I start over?

Settings › Reset progress deletes your hero, stats, subjects and settings from this iPhone. This can't be undone.

Is my data backed up?

FocusQuest has no accounts, so your progress lives only on this iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
